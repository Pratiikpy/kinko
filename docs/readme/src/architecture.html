Deposit address

Vault account

Holds all the USDC. Its key exists only as shares held by Sig Network's signers, and they sign only what the Midnight vault contract asks for.

balance checkable in your browser
Sig Network MPC
threshold signers

Attest and sign

Sweep the deposit into the vault and attest it. Sign the vault's withdrawal transfer. No single signer can act alone.

Relayer

Carries each request through its permissionless steps. It holds no user key and cannot mint.

Midnight (preprod)
shielded KUSD, zero-knowledge payments

Vault contract

Mints KUSD only after the signers attest the matching USDC arrived, burns it on withdrawal, and counts both in public. A mint without the attestation is refused by the contract itself.

public counters: minted, burned

1 USDC arrives at the deposit address  ·  2 the signers sweep it into the vault and attest it  ·  3 the vault contract accepts the attestation  ·  4 and mints KUSD under a fresh nonce  ·  5 KUSD pays invoices, @tags, gifts and cards in private. A withdrawal runs the same road backwards: burn the KUSD, and the signers send the USDC.
